1. EVENT OVERVIEW

What is G-Connect 2026?

G-Connect 2026 is Geeta University's 4-day, internal, hands-on technology exposure and career-readiness event. It is organised around a 3-zone journey — EXPLORE, BUILD, CONNECT — through which students move in small, managed groups across 12 themed stalls.

💡
CORE PHILOSOPHY
TECHNOLOGY AWARENESS + HANDS-ON EXPERIENCE + CAREER OPPORTUNITIES

"Students should leave knowing: what technologies matter, what they can do, where to learn them, what skills to build, and how to connect these skills to careers and placements."

2. THREE-ZONE JOURNEY

The 3-Zone Experience Architecture

The event has exactly three zones with a 5 + 5 + 2 stall distribution, plus Stall 13 (Central Support Desk).

🎫
EVENT SUPPORT • NOT A ZONE STALL

Stall No. 13: Central Registration & Help Desk

Manages participant registration via QR code, verify entries, assign slots and groups (~10 students/group), distribute physical & digital G-Connect Passports, and provide on-site coordinator assistance.

3. DIRECTORY OF STALLS

Explore the 12 Themed Stalls

Each stall follows the 4-step framework: DISCOVER → EXPERIENCE → CHALLENGE → CONNECT (~30 min fast rotation).

4. PEDAGOGICAL MODEL

The 4-Step Stall Experience Flow

Slots are standardized at ~30 minutes. Every stall delivers a high-impact, practical rotation:

1
DISCOVER
What is this technology / skill?

Direct, practical introduction to current industry trends, key concepts, tools, and relevance.

2
EXPERIENCE
Live demo & hands-on activity

Interact with live student-built projects, code demos, AI agent sandboxes, and hardware testbeds.

3
CHALLENGE
Interactive task or quiz

Rapid 2-minute coding quiz, prompt challenge, threat spotter, quantum circuit, or logic drill.

4
CONNECT
Career & Placement pathway

Map skills to campus Centres of Excellence (COE), certifications, internship drives, and placement CTCs.

8. QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

To maximize departmental throughput and hands-on engagement, each day is organized into 11 slots of ~30 minutes each (9:30 AM to 4:00 PM with a Lunch/Reset break from 12:30 to 1:30 PM). Within each slot, students are organized into small groups of ~10 (G1, G2, G3...) for fast stall rotations.

Every stall follows the sequence: DISCOVER (introduction to technology), EXPERIENCE (live in-house project demo), CHALLENGE (interactive 2-min task/quiz), and CONNECT (career pathways and campus COE links).

Upon registration, every student receives a digital passport. As you visit each of the 12 themed stalls across Zone 1, Zone 2, and Zone 3, you collect digital checkpoint stamps. Visiting 6+ stalls unlocks your official Geeta University G-Connect 2026 Certificate of Participation!

Registration is open exclusively for School Students and Faculty of the School across all university schools & departments. Students receive an instant digital entry pass, group allocation, and 12-stall passport, while faculty members receive official mentorship & coordinator passes.
